# Strike review: pay and household benefits

Updated September 22, 2026, in `test-pages/strike-with-support-links.html`. This is a revision to the public review copy; the production `/strike/` page remains separate.

The page now leads with the next OSU payday and explains October coverage using September hours. Our union is the first contact for questions about pay, insurance, timekeeping, food benefits and retirement. Quick answers, the household checklist and the detailed guide use consistent guidance. Continuation coverage is available in an expandable answer for members who may fall below the monthly hours requirement.

Verified sources and editorial distinctions:

- [OSU eligibility rules](https://hr.oregonstate.edu/benefits/current-employees/insurance/eligibility-enrollment-options-and-timelines): enrolled, benefits-eligible classified employees earn the following month's coverage with at least 80 hours of work or paid leave in the month. The September-to-October example applies that rule; unpaid strike hours are excluded. Part-time members are offered individual help without inventing a proportional reduction in the threshold.
- [OSU payroll calendar](https://hr.oregonstate.edu/sites/hr.oregonstate.edu/files/2025-11/paycal20252026.pdf) and [payroll FAQ](https://finance.oregonstate.edu/payroll-frequently-asked-questions): September 30 is payday. This does not establish each employee's check amount or when an unpaid-time adjustment is processed. [BOLI paycheck guidance](https://www.oregon.gov/boli/workers/pages/paychecks.aspx) supports payment of earned wages on the regular schedule.
- [Federal SNAP striker rules](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/subtitle-B/chapter-II/subchapter-C/part-273/subpart-A/section-273.1#p-273.1(e)): preserve the pre-strike and ongoing eligibility conditions, the restriction on increases due solely to lost strike income and the availability of exceptions. ODHS makes individual determinations; our union helps members navigate questions and find food support.
- [PERS unpaid-leave guidance](https://www.oregon.gov/pers/emp/Pages/LWOP-FAQ.aspx): unpaid time does not automatically eliminate a month's service credit. Retain the 11-business-day reporting threshold, other unpaid leave in the same month, ordinary eligibility and nonstandard-schedule considerations. [PERS pension guidance](https://www.oregon.gov/pers/emp/pages/about-pers-pension.aspx) supports the distinction between service credit and contributions based on eligible pay.
- [OSU ACA definitions](https://hr.oregonstate.edu/benefits/current-employees/insurance/affordable-care-act/common-terms): a stability period may provide a different coverage rule. The existing PEBB continuation notice and October open-enrollment reminder remain available.
