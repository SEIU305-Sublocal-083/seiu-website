Start with the need that cannot wait. Find Corvallis contacts below, choose your Oregon county, or ask our union for help navigating a household concern. You do not need to wait until a missed bill becomes an emergency.

Prepared meals

Stone Soup Corvallis: 541-760-6273. The city lists free meals with no identification or qualification required. Meal locations vary by day. [1]

Check the city’s Stone Soup schedule before traveling. Tell the provider about dietary needs, mobility limitations or transportation barriers when you call.

For other areas, use the statewide food finder and ask whether a listed location serves groceries, prepared meals or both.

Housing, shelter and utilities

Community Outreach, Inc.: 541-758-3000. The city lists it as a shelter/housing contact; ask about intake and current options. [1]

Community Services Consortium: 541-928-6335, serving Benton, Linn and Lincoln counties. Ask about the application process for housing or utility assistance. [4]

If you receive an eviction court summons, seek rental assistance and legal help promptly. Oregon Housing and Community Services links to the Eviction Defense Project and local assistance; a pending aid request is not a substitute for responding to a court notice. [5]

Safety and crisis help

  • Immediate danger: call 911.
  • CARDV crisis and support line: 541-754-0110, available 24 hours a day for domestic and sexual violence support. [6]
  • Benton County crisis contact: 888-232-7192, as listed by the city. [1]
  • 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline: call 988, text 988 or use Lifeline chat. [7]

CARDV shelter locations are confidential. Contact the program for directions and support. [6]

Referrals through 211

Dial 211, text your ZIP code to 898211 or browse 211info. Describe the immediate need and any language or accessibility support you need. [3]

Oregon ONE benefits

Apply for food, medical, cash or child care benefits through Oregon ONE or call 800-699-9075. Each program has separate rules; explain the strike, household income and current benefits when asking about eligibility. [3]

Rent and energy bills

Oregon Housing and Community Services directs people to local Community Action agencies for utility assistance and many housing programs. Ask about deadlines, required documents and other referrals if a program is closed. [5]

Make a household plan

Questions to ask before bills come due

  1. Name the immediate gap. List food, rent, medications, transportation and care costs, with due dates. Separate money already available from payments you have applied for.
  2. Ask the provider what happens next. Find out whether there is a waitlist, an appointment or a document deadline. Write down the date, contact and next step.
  3. Plan for access. Ask about language support, disability access, transportation, delivery and whether someone can pick up food for your household.
  4. Tell our union what would help. Contact a steward or our executive team about a ride, an accessible participation option or a hardship question. Ask what is confirmed before counting on an arrangement.

How does this fit with strike pay?

Community services, union strike benefits and unemployment insurance are separate programs. Read our strike pay and benefits guide for the taxable-income correction, application requirements and timing. Do not assume an award from one program establishes eligibility for another.

Support and donation questions: Use the strike hub for current instructions, or email 083execteam@seiu503.org. Ask where supplies, volunteer offers or donations are needed before making arrangements. This guide does not confirm a specific fund, ride or child care service for your household.
