2026 Higher Ed contract summary for OSU classified staff

Contract topics at a glance

Topics covered in SEIU 503’s agreement summary
TopicSummary
TermThrough June 30, 2030; February 2028 COLA reopener, plus five articles each; steps, insurance, layoff excluded.
Union welcomeNew hires: 30 paid minutes within 30 days.
OutsourcingExisting safeguards retained.
Employee filesBetter steward access; supervisory records separate.
DisciplineClearer just-cause progression.
GrievancesStreamlined process.
EqualityExpanded protections, including transgender rights.
Raises3% November 1, 2026; 3% November 1, 2027.
StepsPreserved annually.
Health plans97/3 access retained.
RecallNo repeat trial service.
Sick leaveHardship donations retained.
BereavementNo two-death cap.
VacationJanuary cash-out retained.
LayoffsBumping retained; recall after unlawful immigration detention.
Academic-year jobsSummer PEBB coverage clarified.
ReclassificationSubmitted evidence considered.
Work gearStronger protections; tool-maintenance reimbursement.
WeatherClosures/delayed openings: 48 hours each: 2026–2028 and 2028–2030.
TechnologyJoint review committee.
IT pay5.75% for “meets standards”; 3% for “At/Below” and “Above”.
Shift mealsOSU/UO discounts protected.

Follow-up negotiations

2027 selective-pay talks: Library Techs, Trades Maintenance 1/2, Locksmiths, Pipefitters. UO childcare/PSU access: discussions within 90 days of ratification.
