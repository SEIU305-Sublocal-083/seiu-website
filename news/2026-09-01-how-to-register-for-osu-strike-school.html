Register step by step

The form has several screens. You are not registered until you complete the final step and see a confirmation message.

Open any screenshot to enlarge it. These screenshots were captured Sept. 1, 2026. The state may move items, but use the exact session title and button labels below.

  1. Open the state registration form

    Select Register to begin. You can use the purple Register button in the event card or the purple button at the top right.

  2. Register one participant

    Set Qty to 1, then select Register at the bottom right.

  3. Enter your information

    Enter your first name, last name and home email. A cell phone number is optional. Check the code-of-conduct box, then select Review Sessions.

  4. Select the correct OSU session

    Open the Sept. 9 sessions and select:

    9/9 OSU Campus - Monroe Hall - Strike School

    The title is wrong. This is the correct session, and the event details list Westminster House. Open the session, then select Add Session.

  5. Tell organizers what you need

    Answer the meeting-needs question so organizers can prepare for you.

  6. Finish your registration

    Review your contact information and selected session, then complete the final registration step. Do not close the page until you see a confirmation message.

Ready to register?

Open the state registration form and select 9/9 OSU Campus - Monroe Hall - Strike School. Remember: We are meeting at Westminster House, not Monroe Hall.
