Oregon’s HB 4124 public higher education survey

Oregon’s study looks at student access, regional economies and the financial health of public colleges and universities.

By SEIU Local 503, Local 083

Published November 24, 2026

The survey covers experiences with Oregon’s public colleges and universities, including perspectives from classified staff, friends and family.

Announced survey deadline: Friday, Dec. 4, 2026.

About the HB 4124 study

Under House Bill 4124, Oregon’s Higher Education Coordinating Commission (HECC) is studying how public colleges and universities can improve student access, support regional economies and stay financially viable. EY-Parthenon is collecting survey responses to help inform HECC’s recommendations to the Legislature.

The survey is available in English and Spanish. It doesn’t ask for your name or contact information. It collects IP addresses to flag possible duplicate responses.

Read about the HB 4124 study on HECC’s website.
