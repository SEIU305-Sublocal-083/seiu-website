October 15, 2026 · Your Higher Education Bargaining Team

We Did It – We Have a New Contract!

We did it. Higher education members have overwhelmingly voted (97%) in favor of ratifying our new union contract!

If management had its way, we would have gotten 0% COLAs and lost critical workplace protections. Instead, we stood together, organized, built power and refused to give in.

Together, we won:

  • COLAs: 3% on November 1, 2026, and 3% on November 1, 2027.
  • Full steps each year
  • Contracting out protections
  • Layoff and bumping protections
  • Increased access to new employee orientations
  • And more

This victory belongs to every member who stood together, talked with coworkers, joined rallies and pickets, signed a strike pledge, voted to authorize a strike and prepared to walk out across all seven campuses. We didn’t just win a stronger contract. We built a stronger union.

This bargaining agreement shows what we can accomplish when classified workers stand together.

In unity,
Your Higher Education Bargaining Team
