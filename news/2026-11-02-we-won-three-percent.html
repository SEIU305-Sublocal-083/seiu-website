We won a 3% raise starting November 1

Our ratified contract delivers a 3% cost-of-living increase effective November 1, 2026, for covered Oregon State University classified staff.

By SEIU Local 503, Local 083

Published November 2, 2026

We organized. We stood together. We won a raise.

Our ratified contract includes a 3% cost-of-living increase effective November 1, 2026, for Oregon State University classified staff covered by the agreement.

This increase is separate from annual step increases. Another 3% COLA takes effect November 1, 2027.

Every conversation with a coworker, every show of solidarity, and every member who took part helped build our collective strength. This is a win we made possible together.

November 1 is the effective date of the increase. Check the earnings period and pay rate on your pay statement rather than assuming a particular deposit will be 3% larger.
