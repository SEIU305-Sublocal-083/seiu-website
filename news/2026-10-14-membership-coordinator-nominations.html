Nominations open for Membership Coordinator

Nominations open October 14 and remain open through November 13, 2026, for our Local 083 Membership Coordinator. Nominate yourself or a fellow member who wants to help coworkers connect with our union.

What does the Membership Coordinator do?

Our immediate priority is someone to lead monthly new-member welcome meetings. You will help new coworkers understand our union, find support, and get involved. Outreach strategy and event coordination offer additional ways to contribute with our executive team and organizers.

The broader responsibilities in our posted bylaws, Article 3, Section 6 include:

  • Recruiting new members and retaining current members.
  • Helping develop and maintain the Communication Action Team.
  • Using new-employee and membership lists to reach coworkers and correct discrepancies.
  • Coordinating our union presentation at New Employee Orientation.
  • Annual outreach encouraging nonmember represented employees to join our union.
  • Serving as an automatic General Council delegate and attending General Council.

How to nominate

You may nominate yourself or another member. Send your nomination to 083execteam@seiu503.org and copy Sylvia at sharpes@seiu503.org by November 13.

The button opens a prefilled email. Replace the subject with Nominating [person’s name] for Membership Coordinator, then complete these details:

  • Your name, department, phone number, and email address.
  • The name of the person you are nominating.
  • Why you are submitting the nomination and why this person would be a good fit.
  • The nominee’s department, phone number, and email address, if known.
Submit your nomination

What happens next?

This 30-day notice opens nominations; it is not an election ballot. If multiple people are nominated, we plan to seek member feedback. A formal election is planned for early 2027; further process details will be shared with members.
