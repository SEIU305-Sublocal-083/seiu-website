Find Kerr Administration Building

The identified picket location is outside Kerr Administration Building, at 1500 SW Jefferson Avenue, Corvallis, OR 97331.

Open directions in Google Maps

The map marks the building, not a specific gathering or check-in point.

Plan for access and transportation

  • Ask our team about accessible routes, restrooms, seating and breaks at your assigned location.
  • Check public transit and parking arrangements, and plan your trip home.
  • Arrange care for anyone who depends on you while you are away.
  • If you need participation support, email our stewards about your needs before your shift.

Prepare for time outdoors

  • Check the weather and bring layers, rain protection and comfortable footwear suited to your needs.
  • Bring drinking water, food you need, medications and a charged phone.
  • Save your assignment and organizer contact before leaving home.

When you arrive

Find the picket captain and follow the location’s current check-in and safety instructions. Keep entrances and accessible routes clear, and bring questions or conflicts to the captain.

Members can find the requirements in our strike pay and participation guide. Community supporters should follow the captain’s directions for visitors.

Students, families and neighbors

We welcome solidarity. Before bringing a group, children or a delivery, email our executive team about your visit. Read how to support OSU workers for ways to help now.
