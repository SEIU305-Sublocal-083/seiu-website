How to vote

  1. Find your personal ballot. Look for SEIU 503’s October 5 email titled “Vote on Contract Ratification Now!” Check your spam folder too.
  2. Use the link and unique ID in your own email. Ballots are personal; do not forward your ballot or use someone else’s link.
  3. Save your confirmation email after voting.
Voting cutoff: awaiting confirmation. We are confirming the exact closing date and time in Pacific time. Do not rely on an assumed midnight deadline. Contact our union for the confirmed cutoff.

Missing a ballot or having trouble voting? Contact our organizer or SEIU 503. Not a member yet? Contact us about joining and voting eligibility.
