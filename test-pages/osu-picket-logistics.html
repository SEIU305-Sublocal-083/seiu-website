OSU picket preparation and schedule information

Plan your trip to an OSU picket line: confirm your location, shift, check-in instructions and accessibility needs with Local 083.

I want to helpBack to strike information

“I want to help” opens an editable email to our executive team and Sylv. You can add a personal note before sending.

Meet outside Kerr Administration Building.

Picket location: outside Kerr Administration Building. The building is at 1500 SW Jefferson Avenue, Corvallis, OR 97331. Shift times and check-in details are awaiting confirmation for this guide. Contact our executive team before traveling to confirm when to arrive and where outside the building to gather. Check our strike page for the current strike status before making plans.

Open directions in Google Maps

The map identifies the building. Follow the picket captain’s instructions for the outdoor gathering point.

A strike start time is not an individual shift assignment. Confirm where and when you should arrive, including whether instructions differ for members and community supporters.

What to confirm before you leave

Where and when

Ask for the exact meeting point, date, start and end times, and the contact for changes. Confirm the time zone if traveling from outside Oregon.

Arrival and check-in

Ask where to find the picket captain, how to sign in and whether any sign-out is needed. Members should follow the current participation instructions linked from our strike page.

Access and transportation

Ask about accessible routes, restrooms, seating or breaks, public transit and parking arrangements. Confirm what is available at your assigned location.

Prepare for time outdoors

  • Check the weather and bring layers, rain protection and comfortable footwear suited to your needs.
  • Bring drinking water, food you need, medications and a charged phone.
  • Save your meeting point and organizer contact before leaving home.
  • Arrange your trip home and plan around care responsibilities.
  • If you need participation support, contact our union before the shift so you can discuss arrangements.

When you arrive

Find the picket captain and follow the location’s current check-in and safety instructions. Keep entrances and accessible routes clear, and bring questions or conflicts to the captain.

Members: consult our pay and participation guide for requirements. Community supporters: confirm where visitors should gather and how your group can take part.

Students, families and neighbors

We welcome solidarity. Before bringing a group, children or a delivery, contact our executive team to discuss the location and current arrangements. Read how to support OSU workers for ways to help now.

Tell OSU leadership what is at stake.

Ask President Murthy and the Board of Trustees about an issue identified in our Sept. 18 strike notice and how OSU’s position relates to its published goals. You can add how the possible strike would affect you.

Email the president and trustees Back to strike information

Opens a draft in your email app to the president’s office and the Board of Trustees. Each draft asks about a specific bargaining issue and OSU’s published goals. Review it and add your own experience if you wish before sending.

Email app didn’t open?

Write to pres.office@oregonstate.edu and trustees@oregonstate.edu, or use the president’s contact form. Include how the possible strike would affect you and what you would like leadership to address.

Contact information: President Murthy’s official listing and the board’s contact page.

Get instructions for your situation

Ask for picket instructions

Include whether you are a member or community supporter, your campus or work location, availability and any access questions. For participation support, contact our stewards.
