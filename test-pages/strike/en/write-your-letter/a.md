### Write a letter in your own voice

Tell President Murthy and OSU's trustees why a fair contract matters to your work and your household. If you choose to write, adapt the short letter below in either language.

Before sending, add one short, true example after the opening paragraph: work you do for OSU, a challenge caused by wages or benefit costs, or a protection that helps you do your job. Share only what you are comfortable making part of university correspondence. You do not need to disclose a diagnosis, debt amount or other private information.

Read the whole letter aloud, remove anything that does not describe your experience, and sign your own name. Check that the contract ask still reflects our bargaining team's current position. Your personal paragraph and signature are needed to finish the letter.

**Prepare and review your letter first.** For later delivery, use the [President's official contact form](https://leadership.oregonstate.edu/webform/contact-presidents-office) and the [Board's current contact instructions](https://leadership.oregonstate.edu/trustees/about-board/contact-trustees-and-board-office-staff). The form uses Name, Email Address, Subject and Message. Board correspondence is shared with trustees; do not assume a private exchange.

### Letter text — personalize before use

**Subject:** Support a fair contract for the workers who keep OSU running

Dear President Murthy and OSU trustees,

I am a member of SEIU Local 503's Local 083 at Oregon State University. Our work supports the students, teaching, research and daily services that OSU depends on. We need wages, affordable health coverage and job protections that let us continue that work with stability.

Please use your leadership within the universities' bargaining process to support meaningful wage increases and preserve affordable health coverage and protections against layoffs and contracting out. Help move negotiations toward a fair agreement with our bargaining team.

The people who keep OSU running should be able to see a stable future here. Please respond with the steps you will take to support a fair contract.

Sincerely,
