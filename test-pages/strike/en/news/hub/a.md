Our Local 083 strike information page is now available in English and Spanish. Use it to check the current status, make a voting plan, compare the proposals and find help with money, benefits and work questions.

**[Read our current voting information and next steps](https://www.local083.org/strike/en/).**

Our campaign is about fair wages, affordable health coverage and strong workplace protections. Members also need useful answers about household bills, planned care and what an authorization vote means. The page separates confirmed information from questions that still need an authoritative answer.

Some coworkers could not enter the earlier Zoom call. You should not have to attend a call to get essential instructions. Written information is available on the page, including where to direct a question privately. Questions about money or participation deserve clear answers.

We will use Monday updates for the regular overview, targeted reminders for affected members, and additional messages when a material change requires action. [Get the meeting details](https://www.local083.org/events/2026-09-17-OSU-Member-Meeting.html).
