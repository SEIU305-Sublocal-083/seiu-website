O01 — Proposed ten-day communications and organizing plan

Internal recommendation for SEIU 503 Sublocal 083
Saturday, Sept. 12, 2026 — covers Sept. 12–21 inclusive

Dear SEIU 503 Sublocal 083,

I recommend a ten-day effort built around three connected channels: measured emails with a clear next action, one open local source for current answers, and assigned coworker conversations with follow-through. First, help every member we can reach make a workable voting plan. Then turn the questions those conversations uncover into practical preparation, individual help and our next collective steps. Household concerns belong in that organizing work.

Use each channel for a different job. Email tells members what changed and what to do. The website carries the maintained explanation. Coworker contacts listen, identify access barriers and return with an answer or a specific next check. A weekly email and a stack of flyers cannot do that follow-through on their own.

The case we should make

Our work keeps OSU running. A fair contract must address wages, affordable health coverage and job protections together. In our bargaining team's Sept. 11 summary, management proposed a 1.5 percent cost-of-living increase in year one and 1 percent in year two, a second-year step freeze, removal of the guaranteed low-cost health plan, and takeaways involving layoffs and contracting out. Its four-year proposal has no guaranteed raises in years three and four. Our team proposes 3 percent in each year of a two-year contract and rejects the takeaways.

That dated comparison establishes what is at stake; it is not a signed contract or a prediction of what members will win. Our bargaining team is asking members for authority to call a strike as part of the effort to secure a fair agreement. The decision now is authorization. It is not an instruction to stop work. Members should be able to discuss the proposal, ask skeptical questions and explain practical barriers without pressure to disclose a ballot choice or prove their commitment through financial risk.

H03 should hold the full dated proposal comparison, including the difference between a general wage adjustment and a salary step available to eligible workers. Emails and coworker conversations should use the short case for action and then turn to the member's question and next step. Refresh the comparison whenever our bargaining team issues a new proposal summary; do not carry Sept. 11 figures forward as a Sept. 21 status report.

Four priorities before Monday

First, approve a single bilingual voting block, with a confirmed daytime version that can stand alone. The logistics owner resolves evening and Cascades directions; the election approver resolves absentee and ballot rules. A pending answer must have a named internal owner and next check, not merely a label saying “TBD.”

Second, finish the decision and household package. Use the verified membership application at seiu503signup.org when inviting coworkers to join; explain that signing and submitting the next page completes the application, and separately confirm eligibility for this vote. A functioning signup route does not establish an immediate voting right. H01/H02 explain voting access and authority; H03 explains proposals; H04 separates money categories; H05 covers coverage and leave; H06/H07 cover work responsibilities and participation; Q01 helps members find the right answer. Publishable content should offer the answer we have, its date and a usable help route. It must say “not verified in the sources checked” when that is the gap, rather than claiming a decision has not been made.

Third, assign manageable worksite and shift coverage through our existing Contract Action Team and stewards. Each contact needs a bounded group, a backup, a conversation deadline and a follow-up time. Use the handout to listen and make a plan. Count actual conversations and unresolved access problems. A stack of distributed flyers or email opens does not prove that people were reached.

Fourth, establish accepted response and release coverage. A webpage cannot approve hardship aid, resolve a surgery question or certify a vote. The workflow needs an actual intake contact, subject approver, Spanish reviewer, publication editor and backup. If a shift is not staffed, record the gap internally and do not promise emergency service.

Three connected communication channels

Emails: Use Sunday preparation, Monday updates, targeted voting alerts and the confirmed Thursday reminder. Each message has one primary action. Essential dates, places and instructions belong in the body in English and Spanish. Friday has no automatic all-member email. Send an extra message only for a consequential correction or actual development. If an active strike later requires a different briefing rhythm, announce an achievable schedule after people accept coverage; do not silently turn this plan into daily all-member mail.

An open local information source: After separate authorization and verification, local083.org/strike can become the front door to one maintained answer per topic. Audit the homepage, reusable banners, bargaining landing page, guides, events, calendars, queued messages, Spanish counterparts and short links before describing them as current. Reuse the existing readiness, pay/benefits, support, rights and explanation guides. Preserve dated bargaining stories and source documents; add a dated correction or current-status link rather than rewriting history. Until an approved launch actually works, use the confirmed vote event, Sept. 17 meeting event and Local 083 contact page.

Coworker conversations: The proposed contact assignments in O02 connect the poster and handout to a person who follows through. Ask what matters, what remains unclear and what would make voting or preparation possible. Offer a private way to raise household or medical concerns. Never ask how someone voted, infer a choice from a pledge, or put sensitive case information in the general question list. Optional short video can reinforce the same explanation only after core information is ready, with checked captions and transcripts in both languages. A script draft is not a produced or tested video.

Support and contingent outcomes

H04 must keep six categories separate: earned wages, union strike support, hardship assistance, unemployment insurance, retroactive contract raises and compensation for strike-related lost wages. Offer a household plan based on confirmed resources, with contingent support shown separately. Do not advertise operational hardship aid until amount/rules, approver, private application, urgent triage and payment method/timing are approved. A request for help is not an award. H05 should support individual coverage, surgery, leave and tuition questions without generalizing one member's answer to everyone.

For authorization passing, authorization not passing or a result still pending, use E05/N02's matching variant and an actual authoritative statement. For a notice, distinguish advance notice from an official start instruction. For an official call, put scope, date/time/time zone, work/call-in requirements and the immediate action in both languages in the message itself. For a tentative agreement, state the actual ratification process and explicit effect on any strike call. For a changed timeline, identify the superseded instruction and its replacement. If a strike later runs longer than anticipated, refresh money, coverage, access and continuing/return instructions without promising an end date or funding duration.

Member letters to President Murthy and trustees should request their support for movement in the universities' bargaining process and include an authentic personal example. A01 supplies a starter and verified contact routes; the campaign ask and later delivery need separate review. No guessed president email, invented story or unedited bracket name. A brief public press referral should point to SEIU 503's official contact page; this is not a manager or community communications campaign.

How we will know the plan is working

Use O02's definitions to report coverage by site and shift, meaningful conversations, voting-access barriers, promised follow-ups completed, unresolved urgent cases and matched EN/ES delivery. Show the denominator and unknowns. Keep certified turnout and the authorization result separate from outreach measures; only the election authority supplies those figures. Email opens and page views can describe channel use, but cannot stand in for conversations, votes or readiness.

For the first operational review, leadership should accept or revise the proposed roles and targets in O02, approve the immediate bilingual voting core, and assign the outstanding authoritative answers. Owners, approvers and backups must accept their responsibilities before this becomes an operating commitment. The plan should give each contact manageable work and each member who asks for help a truthful next step.

Respectfully submitted for review.
