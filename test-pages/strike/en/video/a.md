### Complete transcript

Our contract is about wages, affordable health coverage and workplace protections. Our Local 083 coworkers need clear information before deciding what comes next.

This vote asks members to authorize our bargaining team to call a strike. A yes vote is not an instruction to stop work. Signing a pledge is not voting.

Corvallis daytime voting is Monday, September fourteenth, from seven thirty a.m. to five p.m., Pacific time, in the Memorial Union Multipurpose Room. Vote in person, not on Zoom.

Need another voting option or an absentee ballot? The written instructions with this video explain what needs confirmation and how to contact our organizer.

Questions about money, care, membership or work are welcome. You need not share your ballot choice. Budget only with money confirmed for your situation.

Make your voting plan and check in with a coworker. Get daytime details at local zero eight three dot org, slash events dot html.
