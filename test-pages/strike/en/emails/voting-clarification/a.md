**Subject:** A clear voting plan for Monday, Sept. 14

**Preheader:** Corvallis daytime details and help confirming evening access.

If you missed Friday's voting information or could not join our Zoom call, here are the details in writing.

Our strike authorization vote in Corvallis is **Monday, Sept. 14, 7:30 a.m.–5 p.m. Pacific**.

Vote in person at the **Memorial Union Multipurpose Room, 2501 SW Jefferson Way, Corvallis, OR 97331**. There is no Zoom voting at this location.

Our organizer has also announced an evening session Monday, **5–7:30 p.m. Pacific**. Its destination needs confirmation: contact Sylvia Sharpe at [sharpes@seiu503.org](mailto:sharpes@seiu503.org) before traveling. The updated invitation includes members whether or not they signed a strike pledge.

This ballot asks whether to authorize our bargaining team to call a strike. It is not an instruction to stop work.

**Choose your voting time now.** If you need another voting option, membership help or guidance about voting during paid work time, email Sylvia with your question and a way to reach you.
