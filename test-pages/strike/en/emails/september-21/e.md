**Subject:** As our strike continues: check your household plan and support needs

**Preheader:** Current instructions, financial questions and private help for changing needs.

**Our strike is continuing beyond the earlier planning expectation.** The dated briefing below explains the current status and official instructions. We do not have a confirmed end date to share.

Take another look at your household plan. Keep these categories separate:

- Pay already earned.
- Union strike pay.
- Hardship assistance.
- Unemployment insurance.
- Any retroactive contract raise.
- Any compensation for wages lost during the strike.

Track earned pay using your work records and payroll information. For contingent support or compensation, confirm the amount, eligibility and timing before treating it as available money.

If you are claiming unemployment, review the [Oregon Employment Department's current strike guidance](https://unemployment.oregon.gov/strikes) and your weekly claim requirements. Ask the agency how any payment should be reported; our union cannot promise an individual eligibility decision or payment date.

If bills, coverage, planned care or participation needs have changed, contact [083stewards@seiu503.org](mailto:083stewards@seiu503.org) with a short description and any deadline. Ask who can review your case, whether help is available and how to send information privately.

**Review your household plan against the confirmed information below and identify the next answer you need.** Follow only official continuing-action or return-to-work instructions.
