**Subject:** Before you vote: our stakes and your voting plan

**Preheader:** Monday Corvallis voting, absentee steps and where to ask questions.

**Make your voting plan before Monday.** Our work keeps OSU running, and wages, affordable health coverage and job protections are at stake. Our bargaining team's Sept. 11 email explains the proposals.

We are sorry the Zoom capacity limit kept some members out. Here is the essential information in writing.

**A yes vote authorizes our bargaining team to call a strike. It does not tell us to stop work.** A pledge is not a ballot.

- **Corvallis, Monday, Sept. 14:** 7:30 a.m.–5 p.m. Pacific, Memorial Union Multipurpose Room, 2501 SW Jefferson Way, Corvallis, OR 97331. In person; no Zoom voting.
- **Monday evening:** Announced for 5–7:30 p.m. Pacific. Confirm the destination with Sylvia Sharpe at [sharpes@seiu503.org](mailto:sharpes@seiu503.org) before traveling.
- **OSU-Cascades, Tuesday, Sept. 15:** Ask Sylvia for confirmed hours and location.
- **Absentee:** Use the request link in your own Friday email, **“Your Latest Bargaining Update and Strike Vote Info.”** It lists **5 p.m. Monday, Sept. 14**, for requests and **8 p.m. Tuesday, Sept. 15**, for returns; ballots are to be emailed Monday. The email does not state a time zone. Act early and ask Sylvia to confirm it or help with a missing email. Keep your personal link private.

If you are not yet a member, [start your union membership application](https://seiu503signup.org/), then ask Sylvia to confirm your voting eligibility and the signup deadline. Starting an application does not by itself confirm eligibility to vote.

Ask Sylvia about paid-time voting or changing methods after requesting an absentee ballot. Questions about the decision and your household are welcome; [Local 083's contact page](https://www.local083.org/contact.html) explains where to seek help.

We know your inbox is busy. We will keep email focused: Monday updates, targeted voting alerts, this Thursday's confirmed meeting reminder and urgent changes when needed. Local 083's website will carry fuller, reviewed answers as information changes, without repeating every article in your inbox.
