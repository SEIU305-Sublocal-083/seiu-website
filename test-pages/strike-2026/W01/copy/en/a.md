## What we are fighting for

Our wages, affordable health coverage and job protections belong together in a fair contract.

In its Sept. 11 written update, our bargaining team describes management's proposal as a four-year contract with a 1.5 percent cost-of-living adjustment in year one, 1 percent in year two, a year-two salary-step freeze, and no guaranteed raises in years three and four. The team also reports proposals to remove the guaranteed low-cost health plan and weaken layoff and contracting-out protections.

Our team reports proposing a 3 percent cost-of-living adjustment in each year of a two-year contract and rejecting those takeaways. These are competing proposals, not an agreed contract. A cost-of-living adjustment changes the wage schedule; a salary step is a separate increase for workers who qualify.

Bring your questions and your priorities to our union's decision. Doubts about money, care responsibilities or the vote deserve a clear answer.

## Plan for your household

Keep these six money categories separate:

- **Earned wages:** pay for work already performed.
- **Union strike pay.**
- **Hardship assistance.**
- **Unemployment benefits.**
- **A possible retroactive contract raise.**
- **Possible compensation for wages lost during a strike.**

The last two depend on actual terms; neither is guaranteed.

We have not verified approved strike-pay or hardship amounts, eligibility, application instructions or payment timing in the sources checked. Do not build your household budget around an unconfirmed payment. List money already available separately from support that depends on approval or eligibility.

Oregon unemployment benefits may be available to eligible striking workers, with waiting and weekly requirements. Payment is not automatic or guaranteed after two weeks. The Oregon Employment Department decides individual claims and says employer compensation for wages lost during a strike requires repayment of unemployment benefits. [Read Oregon's current guidance for striking workers](https://unemployment.oregon.gov/strikes).

For a bill or assistance question, contact **083execteam@seiu503.org** with a short description, the deadline and a safe way to reach you. Ask who can review available assistance. An operating emergency fund and approved payment process have not been verified for this update.
