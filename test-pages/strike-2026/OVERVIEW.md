**Measured emails.** Sunday preparation, Monday updates, targeted voting information and the Thursday membership meeting reminder. Extra messages depend on an actual consequential change.

**An open local source for answers.** A proposed strike-information hub, focused guides, a dedicated Q&A page and dated news updates. Detailed guides and the Q&A keep references in a section members can expand. Important qualifications stay in the main text.

**Coworker conversations.** A voting poster and handout support direct contact, questions and follow-through. The optional video package includes a script, captions and transcript for later production.

## Review, translate, then assemble

1. Read the English body and item-specific release conditions. Conditional announcements are separate alternatives, not verified events.
2. Add Spanish in the matching translation files, including subject lines, actions, tables and captions. Translate source notes on the detailed guides and Q&A where citations are displayed.
3. Review the final English and Spanish together. Refresh desktop and mobile screenshots of the actual rendered pages.
4. After review, assemble completed item PRs into the translation integration branch in the order shown for their topic stack. Keep the integration PR to `main` in draft until publication is separately authorized and the production changes are ready.

No website updates, redirects, countdowns, email deliveries or schedules are activated here. The screenshots show rendered HTML.

## Sources and scope

The proposed communications cadence is an editorial recommendation. Each item keeps an editorial source record. H01–H07 and Q01 display citations in a collapsed section because they explain voting, pay, benefits, rights or participation. Routine communications and action pages have no citation footer. Dated attribution and important limits remain in the main text. Draft PRs do not settle voting logistics, benefits eligibility, a strike start date or the result of negotiations.
