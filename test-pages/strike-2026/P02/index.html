Before you vote: make a plan and bring your questions

SEIU Local 503 • Local 083 at OSU
Information checked Sept. 12, 2026

The decision: This vote asks members to authorize our bargaining team to call a strike. A yes vote is not an instruction to stop work. Signing a pledge is not voting.

The stakes — our bargaining team's Sept. 11 summary:

  • Management: a four-year contract; cost-of-living adjustments of 1.5 percent in year one and 1 percent in year two; a year-two salary-step freeze; no guaranteed raises in years three and four; health-plan and job-protection takeaways.
  • Our team: a 3 percent cost-of-living adjustment in each year of a two-year contract; rejecting the takeaways.

These are proposals, not an agreement. A cost-of-living adjustment changes the wage schedule. A salary step is a separate increase for workers who qualify.

Plan household money without guessing

Keep six categories separate:

  • Wages already earned for work performed.
  • Union strike pay: amount and rules not verified here.
  • Hardship aid: operation and payment not verified here.
  • Unemployment: individual eligibility and waiting/weekly rules apply.
  • Possible retroactive contract raises.
  • Possible compensation for strike-related lost wages.

The last two depend on actual terms; neither is guaranteed. List confirmed money separately from contingent support. Ask for individual help with money, coverage, surgery, leave or access concerns. For work questions, contact 083stewards@seiu503.org. Start with a brief description, deadline and safe contact method.
