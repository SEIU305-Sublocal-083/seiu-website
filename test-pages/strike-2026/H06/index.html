Get clear answers to workplace questions

Information checked Saturday, Sept. 12, 2026.

Keep accurate records and bring specific instructions or concerns to our steward or organizer before you act. A strike authorization vote does not instruct you to stop work, refuse an assignment or change an attendance report.

Oregon law protects union activity and also sets conditions for public-sector strikes. Whether a particular question, schedule change or disciplinary action violates a right depends on the facts. Our union can help review your situation. See the Oregon public-sector bargaining law and ERB's process explanation.

Can I vote during paid work time?

A rule covering all members has not been verified. Ask our organizer or your steward what paid release or other arrangement applies to your shift before leaving assigned duties. Do not assume that voting is automatically paid or that everyone must clock out.

What if my manager asks whether I will strike?

Save the exact question, date, context and any request for a response. Ask our steward or organizer for guidance on your situation. A possible first response is: “Please send me the question and any required response deadline in writing so I can review it.” This is not a claim that every question is unlawful or that you can refuse every request.

If a meeting could lead to discipline, contact your steward promptly and ask about representation before the meeting. Keep your ballot choice private.

Do I have to work overtime before a strike?

An offer of voluntary overtime, a mandatory assignment and a changed schedule are different questions. Keep the request and ask for contract-specific advice before refusing. If fatigue, workload or safety is the concern, say so clearly and ask how to address it. This guide does not create a blanket duty to accept extra work or a blanket right to refuse it.

What should I put on my timesheet?

Record hours actually worked and approved leave accurately. Do not record time on strike as time worked. Ask payroll for the correct code, submission deadline and alternative submission method if system access changes. Keep permitted copies of your own hours, approvals and pay records to help resolve a missing or incorrect payment. We have not verified a special strike payroll date or universal instruction to stop submitting timesheets.

What happens to keys, equipment and other university property?

Ask for written instructions identifying what must be returned, to whom, when and how to document the handoff. Keep an inventory and request a receipt for anything you return. Bring a disputed instruction to our representative. Do not discard, hide or damage property, and do not assume a general strike announcement resolves your specific responsibilities.

Will work email still be available?

Continued access has not been verified. Give our organizer a personal email address or safe phone number voluntarily if you want a backup contact route. Save personal copies of your own employment records only where permitted. Do not export confidential university, coworker or student information.

Ask our representative which communication rules apply before relying on a work account for organizing. We have not verified either guaranteed access or a blanket ban on every union-related work email.

What about call-in rules, layoffs or retaliation?

Our union's formal strike notice and your individual attendance-reporting duties are separate. Before any official strike begins, obtain the written instructions that apply to your job and shift. Ask how to report a preexisting absence or approved leave.

If you receive a layoff notice, threat or adverse change you believe is related to union activity, save the notice, dates, exact words and relevant records. Contact our steward or organizer promptly. A budget-related layoff is not automatically retaliation, and this guide cannot promise protection from every job action.

When would I return?

Follow our union's official return-to-work instructions for your shift, reporting time, attendance and property questions. A tentative agreement is not automatically an instruction to return immediately. Check the date of any message before acting on it.
