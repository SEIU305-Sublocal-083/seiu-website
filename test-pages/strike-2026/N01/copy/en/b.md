# Make your plan for Monday's authorization vote

Information checked Sept. 12, 2026.

Vote in person on **Monday, Sept. 14, from 7:30 a.m. to 5 p.m. Pacific**, at the **Memorial Union Multipurpose Room, 2501 SW Jefferson Way, Corvallis, OR 97331**. [Check the daytime vote event details](https://www.local083.org/events/2026-09-14-OSU-Strike-Authorization-Vote.html).

This vote asks members to authorize our bargaining team to call a strike. It is not an instruction to stop work. Our campaign is about fair wages, affordable coverage and workplace protections together.

An evening Corvallis voting period is announced for 5–7:30 p.m. Pacific, but its destination needs confirmation because published sources differ. Contact **sharpes@seiu503.org** before traveling for evening voting. OSU-Cascades voting is listed for Tuesday, Sept. 15; confirm current room, hours and time zone with our organizer.

For absentee voting, use the request link in your own Friday email titled **“Your Latest Bargaining Update and Strike Vote Info.”** That email says to **request by 5 p.m. Monday, Sept. 14**, and **return by 8 p.m. Tuesday, Sept. 15**, but does not state a time zone. Confirm the zone with our organizer and act early. If you did not receive the email, write to **sharpes@seiu503.org**. Do not share personal request or ballot links.

Some coworkers could not enter the earlier Zoom call. These written instructions are available without a meeting. Want to join? [Start your membership application](https://seiu503signup.org/), then confirm voting eligibility with **sharpes@seiu503.org**. A new application does not establish immediate eligibility. For membership help, contact **083execteam@seiu503.org**.

For voting-time or other work questions, contact **083stewards@seiu503.org**. Ask for reviewed instructions before leaving paid duties to vote; a blanket paid-time rule has not been verified in the sources checked.

Use our [public contact page](https://www.local083.org/contact.html) for questions and corrections. Make your plan and check in with a coworker about any barrier to voting.
