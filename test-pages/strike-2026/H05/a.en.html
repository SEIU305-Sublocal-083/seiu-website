Check your coverage and leave before changing plans

Information checked Saturday, Sept. 12, 2026.

Get written answers for your own coverage, leave and tuition benefits before making a care or travel decision. Work hours, pay status, plan rules and approved leave can affect the answer. Ask for the coverage date that applies to your household; a general meeting answer cannot confirm it.

Start with OSU Employee Benefits at 541-737-2805 or the official benefits contact page. Ask the PEBB benefits or protected-leave team as appropriate. Bring our organizer or your steward into employment-related questions so you have union support as well as a benefits answer.

Ask these questions about your health coverage

  • Through what exact date is my current coverage paid and active, including my dependents?
  • Which hours and pay statuses determine eligibility for the next month? What premium payment, if any, would I need to make, and by when?
  • What changes if a strike continues into another month?
  • If regular coverage ends, which continuation or self-pay option applies to me, at what cost and deadline?

Ask for the answer in writing and keep it with your plan information. No universal strike-related coverage end date has been verified in the sources checked. At our briefing, a statement about September coverage was corrected to October for people who had earned that coverage; that still does not establish the date for every household.

Have a surgery, treatment or protected leave planned?

Contact your insurer and provider about coverage on the service date, any authorization already issued and any required update. Ask OSU's benefits/leave team how your approved leave and pay status will be handled. Keep approval letters and note who answered each question and when.

Do not treat this guide or a general strike discussion as medical advice to cancel or postpone care. If a coverage concern affects time-sensitive treatment, tell the benefits team and your union representative that a prompt answer is needed. Send medical documents only through the authorized case handler's private process.

What about preapproved vacation or other leave?

Save your approval and ask in writing how your specific leave dates will be handled. Ask separately about preapproved vacation, a new leave request, sick leave and protected leave; the rules may differ. Ask which payroll code to use and whether the arrangement affects any union strike benefit for which you may apply.

The statement at our briefing that approved vacation could not be canceled has not been verified as a rule covering every situation. If management changes an approval, save the notice and contact our steward or organizer for review before assuming the change is final or refusing an instruction.

Put open enrollment on your calendar

PEBB open enrollment is Oct. 1–31, 2026, for 2027 benefits. This enrollment window is separate from whether current coverage continues during a strike. Review the PEBB benefits and enrollment information, including its English and Spanish materials.

Ask now how to complete required enrollment if you cannot access work email or a work system. Do not assume an extension. Keep your enrollment confirmation.

Confirm tuition benefits for yourself and your dependents

Ask the benefits team whether your employee tuition reduction and any dependent benefit continue under your anticipated status, what enrollment deadlines apply and whether an interruption affects the current or next term. The briefing's expectation that tuition would be unaffected is not a written guarantee for your case.

Your next step: Send one specific question with the date you need an answer. For example: “I have approved leave beginning on this date. Please confirm my coverage, premium responsibility and leave handling in writing.” You can start without sharing your diagnosis or private documents in the first message.

For union help, contact Sylvia Sharpe at sharpes@seiu503.org, or SEIU 503 Member Assistance at 1-844-503-7348, weekdays 8 a.m.–6 p.m., excluding holidays.
