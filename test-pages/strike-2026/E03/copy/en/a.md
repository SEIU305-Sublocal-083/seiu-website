**Subject:** Corvallis votes today, Sept. 14: the MU opens at 7:30 a.m.

**Preheader:** Corvallis votes Monday; Cascades votes Tuesday. Absentee requests are due today.

**Corvallis strike authorization voting is today, Monday, Sept. 14.** Voting begins at **7:30 a.m. and ends at 5 p.m. Pacific** in the **Memorial Union Multipurpose Room, 2501 SW Jefferson Way, Corvallis, OR 97331**. Voting there is in person; there is no Zoom voting.

Our organizer also announced an evening session, **5–7:30 p.m. Pacific**. Confirm its destination with Sylvia Sharpe at [sharpes@seiu503.org](mailto:sharpes@seiu503.org) before traveling. The MU session ends at 5 p.m.

**OSU-Cascades votes Tuesday, Sept. 15.** Ask Sylvia for confirmed hours and location.

**Need an absentee ballot? Request it today.** Use the link in your own Friday email, **“Your Latest Bargaining Update and Strike Vote Info.”** It lists **5 p.m. today, Sept. 14**, as the request deadline and **8 p.m. Tuesday, Sept. 15**, as the return deadline, without specifying a time zone. Act early and ask Sylvia to confirm the time zone.

The email says ballots will be emailed Monday; if yours is missing, contact Sylvia for help. Keep your personal link private and follow the ballot's return instructions.

We are deciding whether to authorize our bargaining team to call a strike to help win a fair contract. **Today's vote is not an instruction to stop work.** Our team's Sept. 11 update explains the stakes for wages, affordable coverage and job protections.

Not yet a member? [Start your union membership application](https://seiu503signup.org/), then ask Sylvia to confirm your voting eligibility and the signup deadline. Starting an application does not by itself confirm eligibility to vote.

Ask Sylvia about paid-time voting or an access problem. If you already requested an absentee ballot, seek instructions before switching methods.

**Choose the route that works for you and cast your ballot.** [Review the confirmed daytime voting details](https://www.local083.org/events/2026-09-14-OSU-Strike-Authorization-Vote.html).
