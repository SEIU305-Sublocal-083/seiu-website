Make your plan to vote

Information checked Saturday, Sept. 12, 2026.

Our strike authorization vote asks whether to give our bargaining team authority to call a strike. Voting is not an instruction to stop work. Choose a voting time now, and ask for help if your schedule or location makes voting difficult.

Corvallis: confirmed daytime voting

Monday, Sept. 14, 2026, 7:30 a.m.–5 p.m. Pacific
Memorial Union Multipurpose Room
2501 SW Jefferson Way, Corvallis, OR 97331

This is in-person voting. The Local 083 voting event has the confirmed daytime details. A Zoom meeting does not provide a way to cast this ballot.

Need the evening session or OSU-Cascades?

Our organizer has announced a Corvallis evening session Monday, Sept. 14, 5–7:30 p.m. Pacific. The published location information conflicts. Contact Sylvia Sharpe at sharpes@seiu503.org to confirm the destination before traveling. The Memorial Union daytime session ends at 5 p.m.; do not assume voting continues there all evening.

Our bargaining team's Friday update lists OSU-Cascades voting Tuesday, Sept. 15. Ask Sylvia to confirm the room, hours and access arrangements before traveling.

The Friday update says members may use the most accessible participating campus. Confirm that campus's current instructions first. Cast only one ballot.

Requesting and returning an absentee ballot are two steps

Find your own Friday, Sept. 11 email titled “Your Latest Bargaining Update and Strike Vote Info.” Use the absentee request link in that email. Do not forward your personal request or ballot link to others.

The email gives these deadlines:

  • Request a ballot by 5 p.m. Monday, Sept. 14.
  • Ballots are to be emailed Monday.
  • Return the ballot by 8 p.m. Tuesday, Sept. 15.

The email does not state a time zone. Ask Sylvia to confirm it now, well before the deadline. Follow the return instructions in your ballot email. If you cannot find the Friday message or your expected ballot, contact Sylvia Sharpe. If you already requested an absentee ballot and want to vote in person instead, ask for instructions before switching methods so you do not submit a second ballot.

Paid time, membership and access questions

We have not verified a written rule allowing everyone to leave paid duties to vote. Ask our organizer or your steward for the instructions that apply to your shift before leaving assigned work. Tell them if you need an accessible entrance, language help or another voting arrangement.

The corrected invitation includes members regardless of whether they signed a strike pledge. A pledge is separate from a ballot. If you are joining or your membership is pending, ask Sylvia to confirm your eligibility and any required verification before voting.

Your next step: Put your voting time in your calendar, or find your own absentee email and follow its instructions. Keep your ballot choice private; you can ask for voting help without telling anyone how you plan to vote.

General union help: SEIU 503 contact information, 1-844-503-7348, weekdays 8 a.m.–6 p.m., excluding holidays. A weekend response is not guaranteed.
