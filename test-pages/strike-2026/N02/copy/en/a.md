**Authorization is not an instruction to stop work.** The vote, any required legal process and an official strike call are separate steps. Use the current official work instruction in this update; do not use a possible date from an earlier meeting as a start order.

Our campaign continues to focus on wages, affordable health coverage and workplace protections. Coworkers also need clear information about how any action would affect their households. Approval of authorization does not establish a strike-pay amount, eligibility for unemployment benefits or a guarantee of compensation for wages lost during a strike.
