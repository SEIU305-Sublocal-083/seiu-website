Four days of delegate business that affects every member

General Council is SEIU 503’s highest governing body. From Thursday through Sunday, elected delegates attend workshops, review proposed resolutions in committees, debate union policy in governance sessions and hear from statewide officer candidates.

This is not a general-attendance event. Participation is limited to official General Council delegates and designated leaders.

You do not have to attend to stay connected. Local 083 members can review the planned business below, ask what our delegates are taking to General Council and request a report-back after the event.

Planned schedule, day by day

This is a member-friendly summary of SEIU 503’s published schedule. Times and agenda order may change; “expected focus” describes planned business, not final decisions.

Thursday, August 20

Workshops, orientation and opening session

  • 9 a.m.–7 p.m.: Delegate check-in; board and volunteer activities begin earlier in the day.
  • 1–2 p.m.: Optional workshops on trans inclusion, retiree history, the state bargaining campaign, climate and PERS, and building the union in Spanish.
  • 2:30–3:30 p.m.: Workshops on trans inclusion, retiree history, homecare bargaining, the new grievance tool and building the union; delegate orientation is also scheduled.
  • 4–5:30 p.m.: Official opening, welcome program and first governance session.
  • Evening: CAPE dinner, welcome reception and delegate activities.

Expected focus: Training, bargaining campaigns, inclusion, union-building tools and opening governance business.

Published proposal packet

What delegates may discuss and vote on

These are proposals, not adopted policy. Committee review, amendments and delegate votes will determine whether and in what form any proposal is approved. The official schedule does not assign individual resolutions to a specific governance session.

Bylaws Committee (5 proposals)

  • Candidate access to member records.
  • Article XV election rules and election-process improvements.
  • Making the Civil and Human Rights Committee a standing committee.
  • Creating a permanent Equity and Inclusion Member Advisory Committee.

Employee Representation (6 proposals)

  • A stewards conference, equity training and updated steward leadership terminology.
  • Expanding the Contract Enforcement Team and revising arbitration screening.
  • Establishing an Oregon Workforce Housing Impact Fund.

ESPIA (8 proposals)

  • Union resources related to genocide, ethnic cleansing and crimes against humanity.
  • A climate-superfund program, rights of nature and responsible AI data-center development.
  • Corporate and wealthy taxpayers paying a fair share.
  • War and civilian harm, Cuba solidarity and defending migrant workers.

Member Affairs (7 proposals)

  • Expanding union knowledge and member voice.
  • A general strike, declaring SEIU 503 strike-ready and statewide coordinated labor actions.
  • Code of Conduct training and universal dependent childcare.
  • Inclusion, accessibility and engagement for members.

Union Operations (6 proposals)

  • Establishing a “Crossing Borders” committee.
  • Creating a union website member dashboard.
  • Reviewing IT systems and service gaps.
  • Expanding member access to financial records.
  • Requiring hybrid access for in-person union meetings.
  • Creating complaint, grievance, accountability and records-access processes.
