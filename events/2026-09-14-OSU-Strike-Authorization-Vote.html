OSU Strike Authorization Vote

Cast your strike authorization ballot in person Monday, Sept. 14, from 7:30 a.m. to 5 p.m. in the Memorial Union Multipurpose Room at OSU.

About this Event

Our union is holding a strike authorization vote. Come to the Memorial Union Multipurpose Room on Monday, Sept. 14, between 7:30 a.m. and 5 p.m. to cast your ballot in person.

This listing covers the daytime MU voting location. Check the statewide voting information for other campus locations and updates.
