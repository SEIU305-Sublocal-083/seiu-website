Victory Lunch & Emergency Membership Meeting

We won! No takeaways. A 3% COLA in each of the next two years.

Victory sandwiches outside Kerr

Join us today, Monday, September 28, from noon to 1 p.m. in front of Kerr Administration Building for a victory lunch and emergency membership meeting.

Food is provided. No RSVP needed. Come celebrate what we won together and hear an update on the outcome of bargaining.

The strike is off. Report to work as scheduled. Our agreement is tentative pending member ratification; today’s meeting is a bargaining update.
