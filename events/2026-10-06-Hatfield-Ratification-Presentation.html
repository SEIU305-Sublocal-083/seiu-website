Learn about our agreement. Ask what matters to you.

Our union has reached a tentative agreement. Join us for a 20-minute presentation, followed by time for your questions about the agreement and what comes next.

Members and nonmembers are welcome. Not a member yet? This is your invitation. Come learn what the tentative agreement means for you, ask your questions, meet your coworkers, and find out how to join our union.

What to expect

A 20-minute presentation, followed by 40–160 minutes available for Q&A. We may finish sooner, depending on questions. The end time is not yet confirmed.

These are informational meetings. Ratification ballots are online; there will be no vote at the meetings.
