About this Event

Join SEIU Local 083 members for our August OSU Membership Meeting on Thursday, August 20, from noon to 1 p.m. Pacific time.

Meet with coworkers in Memorial Union room 211 or participate on Zoom. Lunch from New Morning Bakery will be provided for members attending in person.

This meeting is a chance to connect with coworkers, hear the latest from our union, and bring your questions. If you are joining online, request the Zoom link from the Local 083 executive team.
